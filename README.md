### My Projects

- **Classical vs Quantum Otto Engines** — Modelled finite-time classical and quantum Otto cycles to compare their efficiency and power across varying cycle times, investigating regimes in which quantum dynamics can provide a theoretical performance advantage.

  I was inspired to make this after reading this research paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.27376. I thought it was very cool but couldn't make too much sense of a lot of it, therefore used my knowledge of Qiskit to visually display it in a more simple way to myself.

- **Classical vs Quantum Portfolio Optimisation** — Benchmarked classical optimisation methods against QAOA-based quantum optimisation across increasing portfolio sizes, comparing computational performance, solution quality, and scaling behaviour.
